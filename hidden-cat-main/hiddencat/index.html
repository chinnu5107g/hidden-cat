📖 HOW TO PLAY

1
Look at the colored board The enchanted realm is divided into colorful regions.
2
Each color hides one cat BLUE hides 1 cat, PURPLE hides 1 cat, etc.
3
Find the hidden cats using logic Deduce which exact cell holds the feline using spatial constraints.
4
Do not place cats in the same row No two cats may ever occupy the same horizontal row.
5
Do not place cats in the same column No two cats may ever occupy the same vertical column.
6
Do not place cats on the same diagonal Cats on diagonals scare each other away and cannot co-exist!
7
You have only 3 lives Every wrong step costs 1 life ❤️. If lives reach 0, the cats escape!
